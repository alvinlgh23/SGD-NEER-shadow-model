How MAS policy works, in three steps

1

A basket

The Singapore dollar is measured against a trade-weighted basket of its main partners' currencies: the S$NEER. MAS does not publish the weights.

2

A band

MAS keeps the S$NEER inside an undisclosed band around a centre line. It intervenes in the FX market when the currency approaches an edge.

3

A slope

The band rises over time. A steeper slope means faster appreciation, which is tighter policy that leans against imported inflation. Policy changes adjust the slope, width or centre.
